TravelPanel Clipper

Settings

TravelPanel URL

Enter the URL where your TravelPanel app is deployed (e.g. your Vercel URL). The extension opens this URL with ?import=<page-url> appended.

Keyboard shortcut

Press ⌘ Shift S (Mac) or Ctrl Shift S (Windows/Linux) on any travel page to clip it instantly — no popup needed. You can change this in Chrome keyboard shortcuts.

Context menu

Right-click any link or page and choose Clip link to TravelPanel or Clip this page to TravelPanel to save it directly.
