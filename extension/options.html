TravelPanel Clipper

Extension Settings
Local dev: http://localhost:3000  |  Production: your Vercel URL (e.g. https://my-app.vercel.app)

How to use

1. Navigate to any travel blog, YouTube video, or social post.
2. Click the TravelPanel icon in your toolbar.
3. Click Preview & Clip — Claude extracts places and travel wisdom.
4. Click Open in TravelPanel to choose a board and save.

You can also right-click any link → Clip link with TravelPanel.
