TravelPanel Clipper
Extension Settings

App URL

The URL where your TravelPanel app is deployed. Clips will open this app with the URL pre-filled.

Keyboard Shortcut

Clip the current page without opening the popup.

⌘+Shift+S (Mac)  ·  Ctrl+Shift+S (Windows/Linux)

To change the shortcut, go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts and find "TravelPanel Clipper".

How It Works

  • 1 Browse to any travel post (Instagram, YouTube, 小红书, etc.)
  • 2 Click the TravelPanel icon or press the keyboard shortcut
  • 3 TravelPanel opens and extracts locations + substance from the page
  • 4 The clip is saved to your board — ready to plan with
