Keyboard Shortcut

Press Ctrl+Shift+S on any tab to instantly clip the current page to TravelPanel without opening the popup. You can override this shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

How to use the extension

1 Visit any travel page — Instagram post, YouTube video, Xiaohongshu review, or any URL.
2 Click the TravelPanel icon in your browser toolbar (or press the keyboard shortcut).
3 Click Clip to TravelPanel. The app opens with the URL pre-loaded and AI extraction begins.
4 Review the extracted locations and wisdom, then save to a board.

You can also right-click any link and choose "Clip this link to TravelPanel" to clip without visiting the page first.
