How it works

  1. 1 Browse any travel page (Instagram, YouTube, blogs, etc.)
  2. 2 Click the TravelPanel extension icon (or press Alt+S)
  3. 3 Hit Save to TravelPanel — the clip opens in your app where you can assign it to a board
  4. 4 Claude extracts locations + wisdom from the post automatically
