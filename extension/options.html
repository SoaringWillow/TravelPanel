TravelPanel Clipper Settings

The URL where your TravelPanel app is deployed. Must include https://.

When you click the extension icon, it opens your TravelPanel app's Share page pre-filled with the current tab's URL. The app extracts locations and wisdom from the page and saves it to your board.

For local development use http://localhost:3000. For Safari, convert this extension with xcrun safari-web-extension-converter extension/.
