How to use

  1. Navigate to any travel page (Instagram, YouTube, blogs…)
  2. Click the TravelPanel icon in your toolbar
  3. Click Clip to TravelPanel
  4. Choose a board and save — substance + locations are extracted automatically

Right-click any link → Clip to TravelPanel also works.
