## Description (4000 chars max)

TravelPanel turns any travel post into a pinned map location and a
personalized trip plan.

**Clip anything, instantly**
Share any travel post from Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, WeChat,
Bilibili, or TikTok directly into TravelPanel via the iOS Share Sheet.
Our AI (powered by Claude) extracts locations, activities, tips, warnings,
and insider wisdom from the post — in seconds.

**Two layers, not one**
Other apps only pin locations. TravelPanel also extracts the *substance*
behind each post: "go at sunrise," "avoid weekends," "the second floor
has better views." This wisdom is stored alongside your pins and woven
into your trip plans.

**Your inspiration, organized**
Save clips to your Inbox, then sort them into Collections — Tokyo Eats,
Bali Beaches, Weekend Escapes. Each collection is a travel board you can
plan from.

**AI trip plans that cite your clips**
Generate a day-by-day itinerary from any collection with one tap. The
planner reads your saved wisdom and builds routes that reflect *your*
taste — not generic recommendations. Tips from your saved clips appear
inline in the plan with source attribution.

**Privacy-first**
All data is stored locally on your device. No account required. Export
a full backup as JSON any time.

**Features**
• iOS Share Sheet — clip from any app without switching
• AI location extraction with tips and warnings (substance layer)
• Interactive map with clustered pins by category
• Boards for organizing clips into trips
• AI trip planner with multi-day itineraries
• Sourced tips — plans cite your saved clips
• Export plans to PDF or add to Apple Calendar
• Pull-to-refresh enrichment retry
• Swipe-to-delete with undo
• Dark mode support
• Local-first: your data stays on device
