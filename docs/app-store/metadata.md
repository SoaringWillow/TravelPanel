## Description (4000 chars max)

TravelPanel turns your social media saves into real trips.

**The problem:** You bookmark amazing travel posts on Instagram, YouTube, and Xiaohongshu — then lose them when you actually need them. And even when you find them, you have to re-read the whole post to extract the one tip that matters.

**The moat:** TravelPanel doesn't just extract location pins. It extracts *substance* — the tips, warnings, opinions, and wisdom buried inside every post. "The second entrance is faster." "Go at sunrise, not sunset." "Avoid this on weekends." That's what makes a plan actually useful.

---

**Save from anywhere in one tap**
Use the iOS Share Sheet from any app — Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, TikTok, Twitter, WeChat. TravelPanel reads the page, finds every location, and extracts the travel wisdom, all in the background.

**Two layers per clip, not one**
Every saved clip gets two extraction passes:
• *Pins* — GPS coordinates mapped instantly on your map
• *Substance* — tips, warnings, opinions, and must-knows from the original post, attributed to the source

**Organise into boards**
Group clips by destination, trip, or theme. Share a board with friends — they get your curated collection imported directly into their app, no account needed.

**AI trip planner that cites your clips**
Select a board and generate a multi-day itinerary. The AI references your actual saved clips inline — so when it says "Visit the east gate at 7am," you know which Instagram post that came from.

**Navigate live**
Start a trip and switch to GPS mode. The overlay shows your next stop, walking time, and the sourced tips for that activity — right as you're heading there.

**Your data, your device**
Everything lives in IndexedDB on your phone. No account required. Export your entire library as a JSON backup anytime. Import it on a new device.

**What TravelPanel extracts from each clip:**
• Location names and coordinates
• Activities and experiences
• Tips (insider knowledge)
• Warnings (things to avoid)
• Opinions (personal takes from the creator)
• Must-know context (cultural notes, logistics)
• Seasonal advice

Whether you're planning a week in Tokyo, a road trip through Yunnan, or a weekend in Lisbon — TravelPanel is the clipper that actually remembers why you saved something.
