TravelPanel Clipper

Extension Settings

Your deployed TravelPanel URL. Use http://localhost:3000 for local development.

How to use

  1. Navigate to any travel content (Instagram, YouTube, blog post, etc.)
  2. Click the TravelPanel Clipper icon in your browser toolbar
  3. Hit Clip to TravelPanel — it opens the save flow in a new tab
  4. Pick a board and the AI extracts locations + wisdom automatically
