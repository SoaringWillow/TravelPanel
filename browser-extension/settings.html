TravelPanel Clipper

Browser Extension Settings

TravelPanel App URL

The URL of your deployed TravelPanel app. Clipped pages open in this app's share flow. Can be your Vercel URL or a custom domain.

How to clip:
  • Click the extension icon on any travel page to open the clipper popup.
  • Right-click any page or link and choose "Clip to TravelPanel".
  • The share page opens — choose a board, then it's saved and enriched by AI.
