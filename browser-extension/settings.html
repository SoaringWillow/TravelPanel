TravelPanel Clipper

Extension Settings

TravelPanel URL

Enter the URL of your TravelPanel app. The extension will open the /share page on this URL when you clip a page.

How it works
Click the TravelPanel icon in your browser toolbar while on any travel page (Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, etc.). The extension opens TravelPanel's save sheet so you can choose a board and let AI extract locations + wisdom from the page.
