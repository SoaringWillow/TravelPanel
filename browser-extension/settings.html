How it works

Clip any travel page in 2 clicks:

  1. Navigate to a travel post (YouTube, Instagram, 小红书, etc.)
  2. Click the TravelPanel extension icon
  3. Click Clip to TravelPanel
  4. Select a board (or save to Inbox)
  5. AI extracts spots + wisdom automatically
