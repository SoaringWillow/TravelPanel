How it works

  • 1. Navigate to any travel blog, Instagram post, YouTube video, or map page.
  • 2. Click the TravelPanel icon in your toolbar.
  • 3. Hit Clip to TravelPanel — the share page opens in a new tab with the URL pre-filled.
  • 4. Pick a board and Claude extracts locations + wisdom from the page automatically.

Supported: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, 小红书, Douyin, Bilibili, Twitter/X, Pinterest, TripAdvisor, Google Maps, and any web page.
