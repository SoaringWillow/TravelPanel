App URL

The URL of your TravelPanel deployment. Change this if you're running a local dev server or a custom Vercel deployment.

How it works

  1. Navigate to any travel page (YouTube, Instagram, blog, etc.)
  2. Click the TravelPanel icon in your browser toolbar
  3. Hit Clip to Inbox — that's it!

TravelPanel's AI will extract locations, tips, and wisdom from the page and save them to your Inbox in the background.
