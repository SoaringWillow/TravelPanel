TravelPanel Clipper

Settings

The URL where your TravelPanel app is deployed (e.g. Vercel).

How to use

  1. Navigate to any travel post (YouTube, Instagram, 小红书, etc.)
  2. Click the TravelPanel extension icon in your toolbar
  3. Click Save to TravelPanel
  4. Pick a board in TravelPanel — AI extracts spots & wisdom

Right-click clipping

You can also right-click any page and choose "Save page to TravelPanel" from the context menu.
