How to use
  1. Visit any travel page (Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, etc.)
  2. Click the TravelPanel Clipper icon in your toolbar
  3. Hit Clip to TravelPanel — AI extracts spots & tips automatically
  4. Choose which board to save it to in the TravelPanel tab that opens
