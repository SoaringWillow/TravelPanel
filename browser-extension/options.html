Extension Settings

The base URL of your deployed TravelPanel instance (e.g. https://travelpanel.vercel.app).

How it works

  • Click the extension icon on any travel page to clip it.
  • Right-click any page or link → "Clip to TravelPanel".
  • TravelPanel opens and lets you choose a board, then enriches the clip with AI-extracted locations and wisdom.
