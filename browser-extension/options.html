How to use

  1. Click the extension icon while viewing any travel post (Instagram, YouTube, 小红书, blogs…)
  2. Save to TravelPanel opens a preview where Claude extracts locations + wisdom before saving
  3. Quick Save skips the preview and saves directly to your Inbox for background enrichment
  4. Right-click any link and choose "Save to TravelPanel" to clip it without navigating away
