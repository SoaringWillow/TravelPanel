How to use

🖱️ Click the TravelPanel icon in your toolbar to clip the current page.
🖱️ Right-click any page or link and choose "Clip to TravelPanel ✈️".
🤖 Claude AI extracts locations, tips, warnings, and wisdom from the page — not just pins.
