TravelPanel Clipper Extension Settings

🔗 App Connection

Paste the URL where your TravelPanel app is deployed (e.g. on Vercel). The extension opens /share on this domain.

📖 How it works
1
Navigate to any travel blog, Instagram post, YouTube video, or Xiaohongshu page.
2
Click the TravelPanel Clipper icon in your browser toolbar.
3
Click Save to TravelPanel. The app opens and AI extracts locations and wisdom from the page.
4
Pick a board to save to — or leave it in your Inbox. That's it!
