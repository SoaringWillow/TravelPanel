Quick tips

  • Press ⌘⇧T (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+T (Windows) to clip without opening the popup
  • The extension opens TravelPanel's share sheet to let you pick which board to save to
  • Works with Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, Google Maps, and any travel site
  • All clips are saved locally to your device — no account needed
