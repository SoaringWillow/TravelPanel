TravelPanel URL
Enter the URL of your TravelPanel app. This is the Vercel deployment URL or your local dev server.
How to find your TravelPanel URL
New to TravelPanel? Follow these steps.

  • Deploy TravelPanel to Vercel. Your URL will look like https://travelpanel-xyz.vercel.app.

  • Copy the URL from your browser's address bar when viewing TravelPanel.

  • Paste it above and click Save. The extension will open TravelPanel's share page when you clip.

  • For local development, enter http://localhost:3000 (or your local IP).
