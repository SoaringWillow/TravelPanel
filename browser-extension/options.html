TravelPanel URL

The URL where your TravelPanel app is hosted (Vercel, local, etc.)

How to use

  1. 1 Paste your TravelPanel URL above and click Save.
  2. 2 Visit any travel page — Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, travel blogs.
  3. 3 Click the TravelPanel icon in your toolbar, then Clip This Page.
  4. 4 Or right-click any page or link → Clip to TravelPanel.
  5. 5 The clip opens in TravelPanel where Claude extracts spots and wisdom.
