How it works

  1. 1
    Browse any travel page — Instagram, YouTube, a travel blog, Xiaohongshu, anywhere.
  2. 2
    Click the extension icon or right-click → "Save to TravelPanel ✈️".
  3. 3
    TravelPanel extracts the substance — locations, tips, warnings, and local wisdom — and saves it to your chosen board.
