How It Works
  • 🖱️ Click the extension icon on any travel page to clip it instantly.
  • 🖱️ Right-click any page or link and choose "Clip to TravelPanel".
  • ✂️ TravelPanel extracts spots, tips, warnings, and wisdom from the page automatically.
  • 📋 Clips are saved to your board and enriched with GPS coordinates via AI.
