Extension Settings

App URL

Enter the URL where your TravelPanel app is running. This is where clipped pages will be saved.

e.g. https://travel-panel.vercel.app or http://localhost:3000

How to use

1

Set your TravelPanel app URL above and click Save.

2

Browse to any travel article, blog post, YouTube video, or social media page.

3

Click the TravelPanel Clipper icon in your browser toolbar.

4

Hit Clip to TravelPanel — the app opens and you pick which board to save it to. Claude extracts all the travel wisdom automatically.
