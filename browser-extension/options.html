How It Works

  1. Browse to any travel page (Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, etc.)
  2. Click the TravelPanel extension icon in your toolbar
  3. Click Clip to TravelPanel
  4. A new tab opens — choose a board and save
  5. TravelPanel extracts locations & travel wisdom from the page
