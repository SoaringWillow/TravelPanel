How it works

Click the TravelPanel toolbar button on any page to clip it — or right-click any link and choose Save to TravelPanel.


The extension opens the TravelPanel /share page with the URL pre-filled. From there, Claude automatically extracts locations, tips, warnings, and travel wisdom from the post.


For local development use http://localhost:3000.
