How to use

  • Click the extension icon on any travel blog, YouTube video, or social post to clip it to TravelPanel.
  • Right-click any page or link and choose Save to TravelPanel for quick access.
  • After clipping, TravelPanel automatically extracts locations and wisdom from the page with AI.
  • Works alongside the iOS Share Extension — clips appear across all your devices when cloud sync is enabled.
