TravelPanel Clipper

Save travel inspiration from any webpage to your boards.

TravelPanel URL
Enter the URL of your TravelPanel app. This is the Vercel deployment URL or your local development server.

How to use

1
Enter your TravelPanel URL above and click Save.
2
Browse to any travel page — Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, or any travel blog.
3
Click the TravelPanel icon in your toolbar, then click Save to TravelPanel.
4
Pick a board and let Claude extract the locations and travel wisdom from the page automatically.
