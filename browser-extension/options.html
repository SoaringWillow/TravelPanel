How to use
1️⃣
Navigate to any travel inspiration page (Instagram, blog, YouTube, etc.)
2️⃣
Click the 📍 TravelPanel icon in your browser toolbar.
3️⃣
Hit "Extract & Clip" — the extension extracts spots AND substance (tips, warnings, wisdom) from the page via AI.
4️⃣
Save to TravelPanel and it opens in the app, where you can add it to a board.
