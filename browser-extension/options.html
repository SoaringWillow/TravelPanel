TravelPanel Clipper

Configure the extension to point at your TravelPanel app.

Local dev: http://localhost:3000  ·  Production: your Vercel URL (e.g. https://travel.vercel.app)

✓ Settings saved!

Quick-start guide

  1. Generate icons by opening icons/generate-pngs.html in your browser and clicking the generate button. Move the downloaded PNGs into the icons/ folder.
  2. In Chrome, navigate to chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the browser-extension/ directory.
  3. Enter your TravelPanel URL above and click Save settings.
  4. Browse to any travel page and click the TravelPanel icon in your toolbar to clip it.
