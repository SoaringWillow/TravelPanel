💡 How it works

Toolbar button — Click the ✈ icon in your browser toolbar while on any travel-related page (Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, etc.) to save it instantly.

Right-click menu — Right-click any page or link and choose Save to TravelPanel.

Both actions open your TravelPanel app's share screen where you can pick the board to save to. TravelPanel then extracts locations, tips, and wisdom from the page automatically.
