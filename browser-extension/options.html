How to use TravelPanel Clipper

  • Click the extension icon on any travel page to clip it to your boards
  • Right-click any link to clip it directly without navigating there
  • The save dialog lets you pick an existing board or create a new one
  • AI automatically extracts locations and travel tips from the content
