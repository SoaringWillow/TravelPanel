Extension Settings

The URL where your TravelPanel instance is deployed.

Keyboard Shortcut

Press ⌘⇧S on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+S on Windows/Linux to open the clipper from any page. Customize it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

How It Works

  1. Browse to any travel blog, YouTube video, Instagram post, or map.
  2. Click the TravelPanel icon in the toolbar (or use the keyboard shortcut).
  3. Click Save to TravelPanel — a new tab opens with your board picker.
  4. Choose a board and TravelPanel extracts locations and tips automatically.

Safari Web Extension

Convert for Safari (requires Xcode on macOS):

xcrun safari-web-extension-converter browser-extension/ \
  --app-name "TravelPanel Clipper"

The converted Xcode project can be added to the TravelPanel iOS app target.
