How to use the extension
  1. Enter your TravelPanel URL above and click Save
  2. Navigate to any travel page (Instagram, YouTube, travel blogs, etc.)
  3. Click the TravelPanel icon in your browser toolbar
  4. Hit Clip to TravelPanel — locations and wisdom are extracted automatically
💡 Right-click shortcut: Right-click any page or link and select "Clip to TravelPanel" or "Clip link to TravelPanel" without opening the popup.
