TravelPanel Settings

TravelPanel URL

Enter the URL where your TravelPanel app is running — your Vercel deployment or local dev server.

Examples:
https://your-app.vercel.app (production)
http://localhost:3000 (local dev)

When you click Clip to TravelPanel, the extension opens your TravelPanel app's /share page with the current URL pre-filled — the same flow as the iOS Share Sheet.
