TravelPanel Clipper

Browser extension settings

Connection
Paste the URL where your TravelPanel is deployed. This is where clipped pages will be saved.
Loading the extension in Chrome
1
Open chrome://extensions in a new tab
2
Enable Developer mode (top-right toggle)
3
Click Load unpacked and select the browser-extension/ folder from the TravelPanel repo
4
The TravelPanel Clipper icon will appear in your toolbar. Click it to start clipping!
Pro tip
You can also right-click any page or link and choose "Clip to TravelPanel" — no popup needed.
