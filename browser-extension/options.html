TravelPanel Clipper

Browser Extension Settings

App Connection

Enter the URL of your deployed TravelPanel app. The extension will open your /share page to save clips.

Must be the root URL of your TravelPanel deployment (no trailing slash needed).

How It Works

  1. Browse to any travel article, video, or social media post.
  2. Click the TravelPanel Clipper icon in your browser toolbar.
  3. Click Clip to TravelPanel — the page opens in TravelPanel's share flow.
  4. Choose a board and save. Claude AI extracts locations, tips, and wisdom from the page automatically.

Keyboard Shortcut

You can assign a keyboard shortcut to open the extension popup in chrome://extensions/shortcuts (Chrome) or via Safari Extension settings.
