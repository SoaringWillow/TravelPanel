How to use

  1. 1
    Browse to any travel content — a blog post, Instagram, YouTube video, or Xiaohongshu/TikTok post.
  2. 2
    Click the TravelPanel extension icon in your browser toolbar.
  3. 3
    Click Clip to TravelPanel. The extension opens TravelPanel with the URL pre-loaded and kicks off AI extraction.
  4. 4
    TravelPanel extracts locations, tips, warnings, and wisdom from the post — then you can assign it to a board.

Right-click clipping

  1. 💡
    Right-click any link on a page and choose Clip link to TravelPanel to save that specific link without navigating to it first.
