The base URL of your TravelPanel app — no trailing slash.

How it works When you click the extension icon on any travel page, it opens your TravelPanel app's /share page with the URL pre-filled. You choose which board to save to, then TravelPanel extracts locations and travel tips automatically.
  1. Enter your TravelPanel URL above and click Save
  2. Browse to any travel page (Instagram, YouTube, blogs…)
  3. Click the TravelPanel Clipper icon in your toolbar
  4. Choose a board — done!
