Extension settings

Configuration

Your deployed TravelPanel app URL (Vercel, self-hosted, or local dev server).

How to use

  1. Browse to any travel blog, YouTube video, or social post.
  2. Click the 📍 extension icon in your toolbar (or right-click → Save to TravelPanel).
  3. A small window opens — pick a board and click Save.
  4. Claude extracts locations and wisdom from the page automatically.
