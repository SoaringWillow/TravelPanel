TravelPanel / Clipper Settings
TravelPanel URL
Enter the URL where your TravelPanel app is deployed. This is usually your Vercel project URL.

Find your URL in the Vercel dashboard under your project's “Domains” section. For local development, use http://localhost:3000.

How to use
  1. 1 Navigate to any travel post — blog, social media, video page, or article.
  2. 2 Click the TravelPanel Clipper icon in your toolbar (or right-click → “Clip page to TravelPanel ✈️”).
  3. 3 Click Clip to TravelPanel. Your TravelPanel opens and Claude AI extracts spots, tips, warnings, and wisdom from the page.
  4. 4 Pick which board to save to. The clip is enriched in the background — no waiting.
