How It Works
  1. Browse any travel page (Instagram post, YouTube video, blog article…)
  2. Click the TravelPanel Clipper icon in your toolbar
  3. Review the URL and title, then click Clip to TravelPanel
  4. TravelPanel opens, extracts locations and travel wisdom from the page using AI
  5. Save it to a board — and it's ready to plan your trip from

You can also right-click any page or link and choose "Clip to TravelPanel" from the context menu.
