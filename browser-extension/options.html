How to use

1
Browse to any travel page on Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, etc.
2
Click the TravelPanel Clipper icon in the toolbar (or right-click → Save to TravelPanel).
3
Click Save to TravelPanel. The share page opens in a new tab — pick a board and confirm.
4
TravelPanel's AI extracts locations, tips, and wisdom from the post automatically.
