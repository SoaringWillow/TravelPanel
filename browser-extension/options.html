How to use

  1. Browse to any travel post (YouTube, Instagram, 小红书, etc.)
  2. Click the TravelPanel icon in your browser toolbar
  3. Click Save to TravelPanel
  4. Pick a board in TravelPanel and the clip is saved

Tip: Right-click any link and choose Save link to TravelPanel to clip it without navigating.
