How It Works

Click the 🧭 TravelPanel icon on any page to clip it. The extension opens TravelPanel's Share page with the URL pre-filled — Claude then extracts locations, tips, and wisdom from the content automatically.

Works with Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, WeChat articles, travel blogs, and any travel page.
