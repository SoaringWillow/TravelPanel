How it works
Click the TravelPanel icon while browsing any travel post, video, or article. The extension opens your TravelPanel share page with the URL pre-filled — Claude automatically extracts locations and tips from the page.

Works with Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Bilibili, travel blogs, and more.
