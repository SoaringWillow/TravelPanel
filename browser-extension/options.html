TravelPanel Clipper
Settings

The base URL of your TravelPanel deployment. Change this if you're self-hosting or running locally.

How to use

  1. Browse to any travel post — Instagram, YouTube, 小红书, TikTok, or any website.
  2. Click the TravelPanel icon in your browser toolbar.
  3. Hit Save to TravelPanel. A new tab opens so you can choose a board.
  4. TravelPanel extracts locations, tips, and wisdom from the post automatically.

Tip: Right-click any link and choose Save to TravelPanel to clip without navigating.
