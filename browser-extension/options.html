TravelPanel URL

Where is your TravelPanel app running?

For local development use http://localhost:3000. For production, use your Vercel deployment URL.

How to use

Three ways to clip travel content

  • 1 Toolbar button — Click the ✈️ icon in your toolbar to clip the current page into TravelPanel.
  • 2 Right-click any page — Right-click anywhere and choose "Clip this page to TravelPanel".
  • 3 Right-click any link — Right-click a link and choose "Clip this link to TravelPanel" to save it without opening it.
