How it works

Click the TravelPanel icon (or press the keyboard shortcut) on any travel post — Instagram, YouTube, 小红书, Douyin, and more. The extension opens TravelPanel's share flow where you pick a board. Claude then extracts both pins and wisdom from the post — not just locations, but tips, warnings, and opinions too.
