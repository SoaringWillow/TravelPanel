TravelPanel Clipper

App URL
Paste the URL of your deployed TravelPanel app (Vercel or self-hosted). The extension will open your app to save clips.
No trailing slash. Example: https://travelpanel.vercel.app
How it works
  • Click the extension icon on any travel page (YouTube, Instagram, Xiaohongshu, etc.)
  • "Save to Inbox" — AI instantly extracts spots + wisdom and queues the clip
  • "Choose Board" — opens TravelPanel's full save flow with board selection
  • Right-click any link → "Save to TravelPanel" via context menu
