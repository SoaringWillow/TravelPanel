How to use

1
Browse to any travel blog, Instagram post, or YouTube video
2
Click the TravelPanel icon in your browser toolbar
3
Click Save to TravelPanel — Claude extracts locations & wisdom from the page automatically
4
Or right-click any link and choose Save link to TravelPanel from the context menu
