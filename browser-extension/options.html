How to use

  1. Set your TravelPanel URL above.
  2. Navigate to any travel post (Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, etc.).
  3. Click the 📍 extension icon in your toolbar.
  4. Click Clip to TravelPanel — the clip opens in TravelPanel's Share view.
  5. You can also right-click any link → Clip link to TravelPanel.
