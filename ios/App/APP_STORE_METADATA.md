## App Store Description

### Short Description (30 words)
Save travel inspiration from any app. AI extracts tips, warns of crowds and seasons, and builds a day-by-day itinerary citing your own saves.

### Full Description
**Stop losing your travel saves.** Every time you see a great tip on Instagram, YouTube, or Xiaohongshu, you bookmark it — and never find it again. TravelPanel fixes this.

**Save from anywhere.** Share any post to TravelPanel via the iOS Share Sheet. Our AI reads the content and extracts: locations (with real coordinates), activities, and the actual wisdom buried in the post — the local secrets, the opening hours, the warnings.

**Substance over pins.** Other apps extract only pins. We extract the WHY. Every clip stores the tips, warnings, and opinions from the original post, sourced back so you always know where it came from.

**Plan with one tap.** Select a board of saves and generate a multi-day itinerary. The plan cites your clips inline: "Ramen Nagi (from your save: order the Black King)." Real advice, real context.

**Navigate on-trip.** GPS mode shows your saved tips when you're physically nearby. Mark stops as visited, build a post-trip timeline.

**Vibe search.** Find saves by feeling: "peaceful temple at dawn", "cheap street food". AI understands the vibe.

**Private by default.** All your data stays on your device. No account required. Export any time.
