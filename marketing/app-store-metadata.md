## App Store Description (4000 chars max)

**Save travel inspiration. Extract the wisdom. Plan the perfect trip.**

TravelPanel is the travel companion that captures not just *where* to go, but *why* it's worth going — and exactly what to do when you get there.

**CLIP ANYTHING IN ONE TAP**
Share any travel URL from Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Xiaohongshu, or any website directly to TravelPanel using iOS Share Sheet. The clip is saved instantly — AI extraction happens in the background.

**WE EXTRACT THE WISDOM, NOT JUST THE PIN**
Most travel apps drop a pin and call it a day. TravelPanel extracts the full intelligence from every post:
• 💡 Tips — "go before 9am to beat the crowds"
• ⚠️ Warnings — "skip the tourist restaurant on the main street"
• 🧠 Wisdom — "the best viewpoint is actually 200m past the main gate"
• ⭐ Recommendations — "order the chef's special, it's not on the menu"

Every insight stays connected to the source that inspired it.

**PLAN TRIPS THAT CITE YOUR SOURCES**
Generate a day-by-day AI itinerary from your saved clips. Every activity is informed by the wisdom you collected — and the plan tells you exactly which clip each tip came from. No generic advice. Real intelligence from real travellers.

**ORGANISE INTO BOARDS**
Group clips into boards by destination or theme. Tokyo 2025. Bali Honeymoon. Weekend Escapes. Drag to reorder. Share a board as a JSON file for a friend to import.

**ON-TRIP MODE**
Enable GPS tracking and TravelPanel shows you saved pins on a live map. Walk near a saved location and get a gentle reminder — the wisdom you collected months ago, surfaced exactly when you need it.

**YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE**
All clips are stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. No account required. Export a full JSON backup at any time. Your inspiration stays yours.
