Save travel inspiration from any app in 2 taps.

TravelPanel is the travel notebook that finally keeps up with how you discover places. Share any link from Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu, or Douyin — and watch Claude AI instantly extract where to go AND what actually matters: the opening hours, the hidden menus, the "avoid the tourist queue" tips your friends post that every other app throws away.

─────────────────────────────────────
SAVE SMARTER, NOT HARDER
─────────────────────────────────────
Most travel apps save a pin. TravelPanel saves the wisdom.

Every clip gets two extraction layers:
• Spots — precise GPS coordinates, location names, addresses, mapped instantly
• Substance — tips, warnings, insider opinions, and seasonal wisdom quoted directly from the post

When your AI planner builds your itinerary, it cites your own saved clips inline — so you know exactly why Day 2 starts at 7am ("avoid the midday heat" from your own Xiaohongshu save).

─────────────────────────────────────
HOW IT WORKS
─────────────────────────────────────
1. Find something inspiring in any app
2. Tap Share → TravelPanel
3. Choose a board or save to Inbox — done

Claude AI extracts locations and substance in the background. Your map fills up while you scroll.

─────────────────────────────────────
PLAN WITH AI
─────────────────────────────────────
Add your saved places to a board, tap "Plan this trip," and Claude builds a day-by-day itinerary:
• Logical routing — no backtracking across the city
• Your travel style — choose pace, transport, food preferences
• Cited sources — every tip traces back to one of your clips
• Export to PDF or add to Calendar

─────────────────────────────────────
ORGANISE YOUR IDEAS
─────────────────────────────────────
• Boards — group clips by destination or trip idea
• Timeline view — see what you saved and when
• Near Me — GPS sorting surfaces places within 10 km
• Smart search — full-text across titles, locations, and tips
• Data export — download all your clips as JSON (never lose your inspiration)

─────────────────────────────────────
BUILT FOR PRIVACY
─────────────────────────────────────
All your clips and boards live on your device (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded to our servers. AI extraction calls are sent to Anthropic's API — no other third parties.
