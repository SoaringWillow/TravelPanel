## Description (4000 chars max)

```
TravelPanel turns every travel post you scroll past into an organised, actionable trip plan.

CLIP ANYTHING, INSTANTLY
Share a URL from Instagram, YouTube, Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book), Douyin, WeChat, or any website to TravelPanel. Our AI reads the post in seconds and extracts every location, restaurant, hotel, activity, and insider tip — not just a pin on a map, but the actual wisdom embedded in the text.

TWO LAYERS, NOT ONE
Most travel apps save pins. TravelPanel saves substance. Every clip stores:
• Spots — precise coordinates, place names, map pins
• Substance — the tips, warnings, price notes, and opinions the creator actually shared

When you plan a trip, your itinerary cites these sources inline: "Best time to visit: early morning (from your Tokyo clip, Jun 3)." Nothing gets lost.

ORGANIZE INTO BOARDS
Group your inspiration into boards — Tokyo 2025, Paris Honeymoon, Weekend Eats. Boards show a live mini-map of all pinned locations. Share any board with friends via a single link; they can import your entire collection in one tap.

AI TRIP PLANNER
Hit "Plan trip" and watch TravelPanel build a day-by-day itinerary from your saved clips. The AI balances geography (no backtracking), opening hours, and your interests — and cites the specific clip each recommendation came from. Export to PDF or add every event to your calendar with one tap.

ON-TRIP GPS MODE
Activate trip mode to see your real-time location on the map alongside your pins. The nearest saved clip is always shown — perfect when you're on the ground and want to know what's around you.

POST-TRIP TIMELINE
After you return, revisit your journey as a chronological diary. Every clip is anchored to the day you saved it, forming a beautiful vertical spine of memories.

WORKS OFFLINE
All your clips and boards are stored on your device. Browse your saved inspiration on a plane, underground, or anywhere without signal.

DESKTOP TOO
Install the browser extension (Chrome, Safari) to clip from your Mac or PC. Every clip flows into the same app.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your data lives on your device. No account required. No data sold to anyone. AI features use your own Anthropic API key.
```
