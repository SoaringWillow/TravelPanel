Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

TravelPanel ("we", "our", or "the app") is a travel inspiration and planning tool. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data stored on your device

TravelPanel stores all your clips, boards, and trip plans locally on your device using IndexedDB. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use a cloud sync feature (which requires signing in).

Data we collect

  • Usage analytics (optional): If you have not opted out, we use PostHog to collect anonymous usage events (e.g., "clip saved", "plan generated"). No personally identifiable information is attached. You can opt out in Settings.
  • URLs and content you share: When you share a URL to TravelPanel, it is sent to our AI extraction service (powered by Anthropic Claude) to extract locations and tips. The URL and extracted data are stored on your device only.
  • Location: If you grant location permission, your coordinates are used on-device to show nearby saved places. We do not transmit your location to our servers.
  • Camera / Photos: If you grant camera or photo library access, images are used on-device to attach to your places. Images may be sent to our AI service for content extraction only if you explicitly share an image from a social app.

Third-party services

  • Anthropic Claude API: URL content is sent for AI analysis. Anthropic's privacy policy applies.
  • PostHog: Anonymous analytics. See posthog.com/privacy.
  • OpenFreeMap / MapLibre: Map tiles are served by OpenFreeMap. No personal data is sent.

Data retention and deletion

All on-device data can be deleted at any time via Settings → Clear all data. If you have a cloud account, you can request account deletion by emailing support.

Children

TravelPanel is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
